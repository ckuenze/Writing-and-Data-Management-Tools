    Tips for journal submission
    • The figure is built in millimetres, so the exported file is already at print size. Typical widths are about 85–90 mm (one column), 114–140 mm (1.5 columns) and 170–190 mm (two columns). Check your journal’s figure guidelines.
    • Journals usually ask for text of about 6–8 pt or larger at final size, in Arial or Helvetica. Line art is often requested at 600–1200 dpi.
    • SVG and PDF are vector files that stay sharp at any size and can be edited in Illustrator or Inkscape. PNG files record their resolution (dpi).
    • PDF export uses the standard Helvetica or Times fonts. The characters ≥, ≤, ≠, ≈ and − are supported. Other symbols outside standard Western text print as ?; use SVG or PNG for those.
    • The checks panel compares counts across rows and flags totals that do not add up. It cannot know your study design, so treat it as a second pair of eyes.
